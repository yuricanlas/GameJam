# MOONMAXXING

## Description

You are an astronaut who has been hired by the government to clean the moon in preparation for the development of new data centres. While cleaning, you discover unknown life attempting to stop you from completing your mission...

## How to run

1. Open Godot v4.7.2 (which can be downloaded [here](https://godotengine.org/download/windows/)).

2. Click "Import" in the top left of the Godot window.

3. Navigate to the directory that holds this repository's contents.

4. Select `project.godot` and click "Open".

5. Open the project, and the top right corner of the window, click the "Run Project" button (or press F5).

## How to play

### Cleaning the moon

Your main task is to clear debris and trash from the surface of the moon. Approach trash tiles and clean it up.
- You can either clean trash to your right or left. To clean trash on the right of your character, stand on the left side of the trash tile and press `k`. To clean trash on the left of your character, stand on the right side of the trash tile and press `j`.
- Cleaning trash will give you coins.

### Fighting enemies

As you clean up the moon, you'll encounter other life forms that will fight to protect their home from you.
- You can eliminate the inhabitants by attacking them. You approach them and `right click` to attack them with your space baton.
- Be careful! If they get too close, you might pick up their foreign germs, which can drain your HP.
- Eliminating enemies will give you coins

### Getting upgrades

With the coins you collect, you can upgrade your weapon damage, your cleaning tool power, your player speed, and you can also purchase healing items.
- You can make these purchases in your base which also acts as a safe zone against enemies.
